Built for a restaurant, not just a bank check

  • Waiter sends the order to the chef

    The waiter takes the customer's order and sends it from the app to the chef, with the table number. No paper slips, no running to the kitchen.

  • Chef sees the order and cooks

    A new order shows on the chef's kitchen screen right away, so it is clear what to cook. When it is done, the chef marks it ready with one tap.

  • When the food is ready, the chef notifies the waiter

    The waiter gets a notification on their phone as soon as the food is ready, so it reaches the table while it is hot.

  • The app adds up the bill, checks the transfer by QR code and records any tip

    The app adds up the bill for what the customer ordered. When the customer pays by bank, scan the receipt QR code to check the transfer. Cash payments and tips are recorded. No more crowding around asking if it arrived, taking the customer's phone to the cashier, sending money to a waiter's account, writing tips on paper, or getting the bill wrong.

  • Daily reports

    Sales, money out and your top sellers at a glance.

  • A role for each person

    Owner, manager, waiter and chef each see only what they need.

Plans for every size

Normal Verify checks payments only. Restaurant Plus adds orders, kitchen, cash, tips and reports. Prices are shown in the app.
