Privacy Policy

ገብቷል (Gebtual) and ገብቷል Shop · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Gebtual ("we", "us") makes the ገብቷል apps, which help Ethiopian businesses check that customer payments (Telebirr, M-Pesa, CBE, BOA, Dashen, E-Birr) really arrived, and run restaurant orders and bills. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: your phone number, name, business name and role (owner, manager, waiter, chef). Your PIN is stored only as a secure hash; we never see it.
  • Business profile (owner): business type, city, address and, if you allow it, the shop's map location.
  • Payments you verify: transaction numbers, receipt details (amount, date, payer and receiver names and account numbers as shown on the receipt), and the result of the check.
  • Restaurant data: menu, orders, bills, tables, tips, expenses and staff accounts you create.
  • Device: a push-notification token and basic device information, so we can send alerts.

2. Phone permissions

  • Camera: to scan payment QR codes. Images are not stored.
  • Photos: only when you choose a receipt screenshot to check.
  • Location (owner only, optional): once, to save your shop's location so our support team can find you. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Notifications: for verified payments, orders and account messages.
  • Fingerprint / face unlock: handled by your phone; we never receive biometric data.

3. How we use it

  • To verify payments and show the results to you and your staff.
  • To run your account, subscription and restaurant features.
  • To give support, including visiting your shop when you need help.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Banks and wallets: to check a receipt we request it from the provider's official receipt site (e.g. Ethio Telecom for Telebirr, Safaricom for M-Pesa) using the transaction number.
  • Service providers: Amazon Web Services (servers and database), Ethio Telecom hosting (receipt relay), Google Gemini (to read text from receipt images), and Expo (push notifications). They process data only to provide their service to us.
  • When required by law in Ethiopia.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored on secured servers (Amazon Web Services, Europe) with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed PINs and daily backups. Only you, the staff you add, and authorised Gebtual team members can access your business data.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and edit your business information in the app.
  • Turn off location, camera or notification permissions in your phone settings at any time.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or for your account to be deleted by contacting us.

8. Children

The apps are for businesses and are not meant for children under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and tell you in the app about important changes.
